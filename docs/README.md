## What is DayPilot?

DayPilot is your family's calm, intelligent daily co-pilot. Every morning it:

1. **Looks at your calendars** — Google Calendar and Apple iCloud
2. **Checks the weather** — so it can give smart advice ("bring an umbrella!")
3. **Reads your to-do list** — highlights what's most important
4. **Writes a personalised briefing** — a short, friendly summary of your day
5. **Sends it to your phone** — via a push notification before you start your day

The dashboard stays open all day so you can always answer the question:
> *"What do I need to know and do today?"*
