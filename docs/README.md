## What is DayPilot?

DayPilot is a **self-hosted, AI-powered family planning system** that runs on your home server. Every morning it:

1. **Syncs your calendars** — Google Calendar and Apple iCloud, merged into one view
2. **Checks the weather** — and uses it to give smart, contextual advice
3. **Reads your to-do list** — highlights what matters most
4. **Writes an AI-powered briefing** — a short, friendly summary of your day
5. **Pushes it to your phone** — via a push notification before your day starts

The dashboard stays available all day so anyone in the family can always answer:
> *"What do we need to know and do today?"*
